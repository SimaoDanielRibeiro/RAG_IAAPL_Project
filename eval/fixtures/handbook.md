# Company Handbook

## Project Submission Deadlines

All projects must be submitted by March 15, 2026. Late submissions
will not be accepted without prior written approval from the
program director.

## Code Review Process

Pull requests require approval from at least two senior engineers
before merging to the main branch. Engineering managers may approve
their own team's pull requests in emergency situations.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. The remaining
two days must be spent at the office to support collaboration and
mentorship.

## Travel Reimbursement

Business travel expenses are reimbursed within 14 days of receipt
submission. Receipts older than 60 days are not eligible for
reimbursement.
