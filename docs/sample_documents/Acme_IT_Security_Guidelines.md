# Acme Robotics - IT Security Guidelines

Version 2.1, effective 1 February 2026. Owner: IT Security team.
Fictional company. Sample document created to demonstrate a document Q&A assistant.

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines protect Acme Robotics' people, customers and intellectual property, including robot firmware, fleet-management software and customer warehouse data. They apply to every employee, contractor and intern who uses a company device or account.

## 2. Accounts and Passwords

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every company account. Use the authenticator app; SMS codes are only allowed as a temporary backup.
- Store all work passwords in the company password manager. Never reuse a work password on any other website.
- Passwords must have at least 14 characters. A passphrase of four or more random words is recommended.
- Never share your password or MFA codes with anyone, including colleagues and IT staff. IT will never ask for them.

## 3. Devices

- Company laptops are encrypted with full-disk encryption, and encryption must never be disabled.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lock your screen manually (Windows + L or Ctrl + Cmd + Q) whenever you leave your desk.
- Install software only through the Self Service portal. Browser extensions must be on the approved list.
- Do not copy company data to personal USB drives or personal cloud storage. Use the company file storage for all work documents.

## 4. Working Remotely and Travelling

- Always connect through the company VPN when using public or hotel Wi-Fi networks.
- When travelling, keep your laptop in your carry-on luggage. Never check it in or leave it unattended in a car.
- Use a privacy screen filter when working in trains, planes or other public places.
- Before travelling outside the European Union, contact IT at least 5 working days in advance. Some countries require a loaner laptop with limited data.

## 5. Phishing and Suspicious Messages

- Report suspicious emails within 1 hour using the "Report phishing" button in your email client, or forward them to security@acme-robotics.example.
- Do not click links or open attachments in messages you were not expecting, even if they appear to come from a colleague.
- If you clicked a suspicious link or entered your password on an unknown page, report it immediately. Fast reporting matters more than blame.

## 6. Lost or Stolen Devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 2 hours to the IT Service Desk (+351 210 000 000, available 24/7). IT will lock and remotely wipe the device. You must also file a police report within 48 hours if the device was stolen.

## 7. Incident Severity Levels

| Level | Example | Response time |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Critical | Ransomware, customer data leak | 15 minutes, 24/7 |
| High | Compromised account, stolen laptop | 1 hour |
| Medium | Malware blocked by antivirus | 4 working hours |
| Low | Suspicious email reported, no click | 1 working day |
