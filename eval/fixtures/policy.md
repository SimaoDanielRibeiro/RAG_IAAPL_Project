# Security Policy

## Safety Audits

The compliance officer is responsible for conducting quarterly
safety audits. The audit results must be submitted to the
executive committee within 30 days of completion.

## Access Control

All production systems require two-factor authentication.
Access requests must be approved by the security team lead before
credentials are issued.

## Incident Response

In the event of a security incident, contact the on-call engineer
immediately at the emergency line. Document the incident in the
incident tracker within 24 hours of detection.

## Data Retention

Customer data is retained for a maximum of seven years from the date
of the last interaction. Anonymized analytics data may be retained
indefinitely for trend analysis.
