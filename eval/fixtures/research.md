# Q3 Research Findings

## Customer Satisfaction Survey

Our Q3 customer satisfaction score reached 87 percent, an improvement
of 5 percentage points from Q2. Response rate was 42 percent across
2,150 surveyed customers.

## Net Promoter Score

The Net Promoter Score increased from 41 in Q2 to 48 in Q3, the
largest single-quarter gain in the program's history.

## Key Findings

- 73 percent of customers cited product reliability as the top reason
  for satisfaction
- Support response time averaged 4.2 hours, down from 6.1 hours in Q2
- 18 percent of detractors mentioned pricing concerns as the primary
  driver of low scores

## Recommended Actions

The customer success team should prioritize outreach to detractors
who cited pricing, and engineering should continue investing in
reliability improvements identified as the top satisfaction driver.
